# Investigating Yuka’s Food Scores

An independent research and writing project by Isabella Doan.

## About this project

My article, “Yuka Just Rated My Protein Bar ‘Bad,’ but the Barcode
Couldn’t Really Tell Me Why,” examines how Yuka turns ingredient lists
and nutrition labels into a single score—and what that score leaves out.

Published on Medium on September 18, 2026.

## What I did

- Scanned products from my own pantry and grocery cart.
- Examined Yuka’s scoring weights and additive penalties.
- Compared per-100-gram calculations with ordinary serving sizes.
- Cross-checked explanations against scientific studies and regulatory sources.
- Created original graphics explaining the scoring system.
- Documented my sources, methods, and limitations.

## Main question

What can a barcode-scanning app tell us about a food, and what can’t
it determine without knowing ingredient amounts and actual consumption?
